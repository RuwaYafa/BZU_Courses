# Necessary and Sufficient Conditions - Questions and Answers

---

### **Section 1: General Knowledge Examples**

#### **Question 1**
**Is being a bat necessary or sufficient to be a flying mammal?**

* **Answer:**
  1. **It is necessary:** Bats are the only mammal that can fly.
  2. **It is sufficient:** Bats can fly, and are mammals.

---

#### **Question 2**
**Is playing cricket necessary or sufficient to understand all of the laws of cricket?**

* **Answer:**
  1. **It is not necessary:** You can understand and know all the rules of cricket without playing it.
  2. **It is not sufficient:** You can play cricket happily without knowing or understanding all of the laws.

---

#### **Question 3**
**Is it necessary or sufficient to be a twin in order to have a sibling?**

* **Answer:**
  1. **It is not necessary:** You can have a sibling without being a twin.
  2. **It is sufficient:** If you are a twin you have a sibling.

---

#### **Question 4**
**Is it necessary or sufficient to bring a passport to the airport to get on an international flight?**

* **Answer:**
  1. **It is necessary:** They will not allow you on the flight without one.
  2. **It is not sufficient:** You also need to: Pay for the flight, turn up at the right time, visa etc.

---

#### **Question 5**
**Is being Chris Hemsworth necessary or sufficient to be beautiful?**

* **Answer:**
  1. **It is sufficient:** If you are Chris Hemsworth, you are beautiful.
  2. **It is not necessary:** You could be someone/something else to be beautiful.

---

#### **Question 6**
**Is being an adult necessary or sufficient to be a parent?**

* **Answer:**
  1. **It is not necessary:** You could be a minor and still be a parent.
  2. **It is not sufficient:** Lots of adults do not have children.

---

#### **Question 7**
**Is being from Birmingham necessary or sufficient to be British?**

* **Answer:**
  1. **It is sufficient:** If you are from Birmingham, you are British.
  2. **It is not necessary:** You could also be from somewhere else.

---

#### **Question 8**
**Is being a parent necessary or sufficient to have a daughter?**

* **Answer:**
  1. **It is necessary:** The only people with daughters are parents.
  2. **It is not sufficient:** You could be a parent of only sons.

---

#### **Question 9**
**Is being an adult necessary or sufficient to be over 18 (UK)?**

* **Answer:**
  1. **It is necessary:** You are not considered an adult until 18.
  2. **It is sufficient:** This is the only criteria that needs to be met. Once you are 18 you are an adult.

---

### **Section 2: Mathematical & Geometrical Examples**

#### **Question 10**
**Is having 4 equal straight sides necessary or sufficient to be a square?**

* **Answer:**
  1. **It is necessary:** Squares have 4 equal straight sides.
  2. **It is not sufficient:** It also needs 4 right angles.

---

#### **Question 11**
**Is being prime necessary or sufficient to be a positive integer?**

* **Answer:**
  1. **It is not necessary:** 4 (is a positive integer but not prime).
  2. **It is sufficient:** Primes are positive integers.

---

#### **Question 12**
**Is being prime necessary or sufficient to have 2 factors?**

* **Answer:**
  1. **It is necessary:** The only numbers with 2 factors are prime.
  2. **It is sufficient:** Primes all have 2 factors.

---

#### **Question 13**
**Is a number being less than 12 necessary or sufficient to be less than 20?**

* **Answer:**
  1. **It is sufficient:** If you are less than 12 you are also less than 20.
  2. **It is not necessary:** You could be 19.

---

#### **Question 14**
**Is being a square necessary or sufficient to be a rectangle?**

* **Answer:**
  1. **It is not necessary:** You could be a rectangle but not be a square.
  2. **It is sufficient:** If you are a square then you are a rectangle.

---

#### **Question 15**
**Is being odd necessary or sufficient to be prime?**

* **Answer:**
  1. **It is not sufficient:** You must only have 2 factors.
  2. **It is not necessary:** You could be 2.

---

#### **Question 16**
**Is being less than 20 necessary or sufficient to be more than 15?**

* **Answer:**
  1. **It is not necessary:** You could be 25.
  2. **It is not sufficient:** You could be 14.

---

#### **Question 17**
**Is being a quadrilateral necessary or sufficient to be a square?**

* **Answer:**
  1. **It is necessary:** Squares must have 4 sides.
  2. **It is not sufficient:** There are lots of quadrilaterals that don’t have 4 equal sides and 4 right angles.
